## Reference images and videos

Cliponaut may allow you to provide a reference image or video so that you can
ask us to analyze visual characteristics such as colour, typography, layout,
and animation treatment while editing your own footage. A reference is used as
stylistic input; it is not made an output source, soundtrack, or replacement
for your footage.

You are responsible for ensuring that your upload and your requested use have
the rights, permissions, licences, consent, or applicable copyright exceptions
required where you are located and where you use Cliponaut. We do not require
you to prove ownership for every reference, and we do not automatically reject
reference media solely because it was published by another creator, appears to
be a screen recording, or may be copyrighted. That does not make an upload or
use lawful.

Do not upload reference media or request edits that are unlawful, infringing,
or prohibited by our Content Safety Policy. Do not use Cliponaut to download,
scrape, or bypass the rules of another service. You must obtain reference media
lawfully before uploading it to Cliponaut.

## Complaints and removal

If you believe material on Cliponaut infringes your rights or is prohibited,
contact us at [cliponauthq@gmail.com](mailto:cliponauthq@gmail.com) with enough
information for us to review the request. Cliponaut may restrict access, reject
or remove material, and preserve information where reasonably necessary to
investigate reports, protect users, or comply with applicable law.
